# 🎮 QuizQuest

> Turn any syllabus into an epic RPG battle where knowledge is your weapon.

## Overview

**QuizQuest** is a browser-based educational RPG that transforms any syllabus into a personalized battle game. Students upload a PDF of their study material, and AI instantly generates a custom quiz before dropping them into a combat arena where learning drives every attack.

No pre-built question banks. No manual setup. Every adventure is uniquely generated from the material you provide.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Upload a syllabus or study material PDF.
2. The system extracts the content.
3. AI analyzes the material and generates **20 custom questions**.
4. Questions are distributed across **four difficulty tiers**:
   - Easy
   - Medium
   - Hard
   - Expert

5. Enter the battlefield and defeat enemies by answering questions correctly.

Every game session is generated from the uploaded content, ensuring a personalized learning experience.

---

## ⚔️ Gameplay

You face enemies in a battle arena where every attack is a question.

- The enemy attacks by throwing a question at your character.
- Four answer options appear as counter-attack choices.
- Choose the correct answer to damage the enemy.
- Choose incorrectly and the attack rebounds, damaging your character instead.

### Progression System

Progress must be earned.

- Questions unlock sequentially.
- The next question becomes available only after the current one is answered correctly.
- Players cannot skip difficult questions.
- Mastery is achieved through understanding, not guessing.

Each topic contains four stages, each protected by a different enemy.

Defeat all enemies to fully master the topic.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Generated Quizzes

Generate quizzes from any uploaded syllabus or study material.

- Supports any subject
- Works for grades 1–10
- Compatible with any curriculum

### ⚔️ Combat-Based Learning

Questions become challenges to overcome rather than tests to endure.

### 🎲 Endless Replayability

Enemies, assets, and encounters are randomized each session.

### 📱 Mobile-First Design

Built primarily for portrait-mode mobile devices while remaining playable in any modern browser.

### 💡 Instant Feedback

Incorrect answers display explanations before gameplay continues, transforming mistakes into learning opportunities.

### 📈 Mastery Tracking

Completed topics are saved so students can track their learning progress and achievements.
